# ELCEO Editorial Standard

## Voice

ELCEO should sound market-literate, precise, calm, diagnostic, and serious. Public copy should make a trader feel that the product understands the structure of their problem rather than selling hype.

## Product framing

ELCEO is market intelligence and decision support. It is not:

- a broker or execution venue;
- a deposit/custody product;
- a signal-selling service;
- an investment adviser;
- a guarantee of profit, certainty, or successful trades.

## Preferred vocabulary

Use concrete language such as:

- context
- evidence
- pressure
- contradiction
- freshness
- scenario
- selectivity
- review
- market structure
- tracked markets
- decision context
- confidence context
- market routine
- headline shock
- stale narrative
- weak zone significance
- single-timeframe bias
- base/quote misread
- unreviewed pattern repetition

## Restricted/avoid language

Do not present ELCEO as telling the user to buy/sell or as guaranteeing outcomes. Avoid wording such as:

- guaranteed / risk-free / sure win
- beat the market
- predict with certainty
- never lose
- profitable signals
- execute your trade through ELCEO
- deposit funds / withdraw funds from ELCEO
- AI will make the decision for you

If a phrase could reasonably imply brokerage, execution, custody, personalized investment advice, or guaranteed financial results, rewrite it.

## Public-site copy

Keep copy concise and concrete. Teach through recognizable trader problems rather than exposing internal engine names.

Prefer:

`Headline shock` / `Stale narrative` / `Weak zone significance` / `Single-timeframe bias`

over abstract marketing such as:

`AI-powered intelligence revolution` / `next-generation trading edge`.

Do not overload the landing page with feature inventory. Public storytelling should reveal why context matters and what common decision flaws look like; deeper functionality belongs inside the application or dedicated product pages.

## Typography/case

Store copy in natural case and style presentation in CSS. Avoid all-caps meta labels everywhere. Small labels/eyebrows may be uppercase when composition specifically calls for them, but they are not the default component pattern.

## CTA language

Primary landing CTA should promise entry into the product experience, not implementation details of OAuth. Examples for evaluation include:

- Start analyzing the market
- Start exploring ELCEO
- Build your market routine

Google remains the authentication/signup mechanism and may be stated on the signup/auth surface.

## Dashboard/application

The application may use more technical ELCEO vocabulary because users are already inside the product, but must still distinguish server-produced cognition from user action. Never turn bias/confidence/context into an execution command.
